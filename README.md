# PDEs in Finance

My solutions to the lab sessions of the PDEs in finance course at Ensimag in Python. The course has two lab sessions, and the second one will be added once completed.

## Contents

| Notebook | Topic | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `finite_differences_barrier_options.ipynb` | Finite differences | Solves the heat equation with absorbing boundaries using the explicit, implicit and Crank-Nicolson schemes. Studies stability (Von Neumann analysis, stability map), convergence orders and accuracy vs cost, compares with Monte Carlo, and prices a double barrier knock-out call under Black-Scholes. |


The exercise statements (énoncés) come from the course.
